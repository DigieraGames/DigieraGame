Privacy Policy

Sparkade

Sparkade: Offline & 2 Player, by Digiera Games
Effective date: 20 November 2026

  • Plays offline
  • Ads by Google AdMob
  • Optional Google sign-in

In short: the core games work offline and your progress is stored on your device. Online features (sign-in, friend connections, ads and purchases) use Google and Firebase services, described below. Contact: digieragames@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

Core games can be played offline. The app stores settings, game progress, scores, Sparks, unlocks and purchase-delivery records on your device. Clearing app storage may remove local progress. Device backup behavior may depend on Android and your settings.

If you use Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes account information such as your user identifier, name, email address and profile image when provided. The app can read account-linked entitlements. Multiplayer room pairing can also use an anonymous Firebase account. Signing out ends your session; it does not itself delete your account.

Supported online multiplayer uses connection and signaling data, room codes and network addresses to connect players through Firebase, WebRTC, STUN and, when configured, relay providers. Game input, state or streamed game pictures and audio are sent to the other player as needed for the selected connection mode.

Analytics, advertising and purchases

The Android app uses Firebase Analytics for usage events such as games played, modes and session outcomes. You can turn usage statistics off in Settings > Privacy. Analytics SDKs may process device and app identifiers and technical information; game event payloads do not intentionally include your name or email.

The app offers Google AdMob advertisements. Advertising services may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostic information to provide ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. Consent and ad privacy choices are presented where required by the configured service. Availability of ads depends on connectivity and eligibility. The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns ads off.

Google Play handles purchase checkout. The app uses product identifiers and purchase or transaction tokens to deliver Sparks and Remove Ads, restore eligible purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. The app does not receive payment card numbers from Google Play.

Support and service providers

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Service providers process information for authentication, advertising, analytics, purchases, hosting and multiplayer connectivity. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local data remains until you remove it through app or device controls, subject to backup behavior. Records held by Google, Firebase and other providers follow those providers' retention practices. Deleting local storage or signing out alone does not delete provider-held records.

To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of your data, email digieragames@gmail.com. Include the Google account used to sign in, if any, so we can find your records.

Security, children and changes

Network services use their configured security protections. No system can guarantee absolute security. Sparkade is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated as services change. The effective date above shows the latest version.
