Account deletion

Delete your Sparkade account

For the Android app Sparkade: Offline & 2 Player, by Digiera Games.

  • In the app
  • Or by email
  • No password needed here

Note: you sign in to Sparkade with Google. The same account is shared with our other app, Gamepad Tester, so deleting it deletes it for both apps.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Sparkade.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account & data.
  3. Confirm, then sign in with Google again to prove it is you.

The account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Send an email to digieragames@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Sparkade account.

Send it from the Google email address you signed in with, because that is how we confirm it is you. We may reply to verify before deleting. We complete the request within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: the Google name, email and profile picture as stored by us.
  • The account data stored with it, including the record of purchases linked to the account.

What is kept

  • Purchases made through Google Play stay with Google, and Google keeps its own payment records. Remove Ads can be restored with Restore purchases in the app's Shop.
  • Progress, Sparks and settings saved only on your phone are not on our servers. They stay until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are not linked to your account. Google keeps them for the retention period set in Analytics.
  • We may keep information we are legally required to keep.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests, and any records the app cannot remove itself, are deleted within 30 days.

Questions?

Email digieragames@gmail.com. Read the full Sparkade privacy policy.
