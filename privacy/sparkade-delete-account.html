Account deletion

Delete your Sparkade account

For the Android app Sparkade: Offline & 2 Player, by Digiera Games.

  • In the app
  • Or by email
  • No password needed here

In the app

If you signed in with Google

  1. Open Sparkade and go to Settings, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete account & data and confirm.
  3. Sign in with Google again to prove it is you. The account is deleted straight away.

The same account is used by Gamepad Tester, so it is deleted for that app too.

If you only created a username (no Google sign-in)

  1. Open Sparkade and go to Friends.
  2. Tap Options, then Delete username, and confirm.

By email (no app needed)

Email digieragames@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Sparkade account.

  • If you signed in with Google, send it from that Google email address.
  • If you have a username, include it. We may ask you questions to confirm it is yours.

We complete requests within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account.
  • Your username, friend requests, friendships and block list.
  • The game progress saved to your account, and other account data, including the record of purchases linked to it.

Automatic deletion

If you have not signed in with Google, your username and its data are deleted after 30 days without opening the app. An anonymous identifier without your name or email address may remain.

What is kept

  • Reports about players, kept for safety review for up to 6 months.
  • Purchases made through Google Play, and Google's payment records. Restore purchases in the Shop brings Remove Ads back.
  • Data saved only on your phone, until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Anonymous usage statistics, which are not linked to your account and are kept for the period set in Google Analytics.
  • Information we are legally required to keep.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests, and any records the app cannot remove itself, are deleted within 30 days, except for the information listed under "What is kept".
